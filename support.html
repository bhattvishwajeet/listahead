ListAhead

A grocery list that learns how you shop. It organizes your list by store, shows you where to start, and suggests what you might need next.

Start at

Your list, organized by store.

Give each item a preferred store, and your list groups itself by store. Start at picks the store that carries the most of what you need, so you know where to go first.

Might need soon

Know you’re running low before you run out.

ListAhead learns how often you buy each item (milk every 4 days, coffee every 2 weeks) and suggests what’s due. Check the ones you need and add them to your list in one tap.

Available here

Shop smarter. Make fewer stops.

Add the other stores that carry an item under Also available at. While you shop at one store, Available here shows items from your list that it also carries, so you can skip the next stop.

Check items off as they go in the cart. Finish shopping saves them to Purchase History; anything you didn’t get stays on the list for next time.

Purchase History

Your shopping habits, at a glance.

Every trip you finish shows what you buy and how often: your trips, items purchased, the things you buy most and their purchase patterns.

Just for fun

Beyond groceries. Stay on top of chores.

Add a “store” called Household Chores and check chores off like groceries. ListAhead learns how often each one comes around.

How it works

Home

  • Tap a store to see just its list.
  • Start at picks the store with most of your list.
  • Might need soon suggests items you buy regularly.
  • No match in search? Add it straight from there.
  • Tap an item to pick a quantity first.
  • Touch and hold an item to edit it.
  • A checkmark means it’s already on your list.
  • Adding an item with no store asks for one first.

Items & Stores

  • Preferred Store sets where an item goes on your list.
  • Also available at lists it under more stores.
  • Stores show on Home once they have items.
  • Start from the built-in catalog of common groceries, or type your own.
  • Popular US stores are already set up. Rename or archive them anytime.

Shopping List

  • Pick a store to shop just that store.
  • Available here: items from other stores this one carries.
  • Finish shopping saves what’s checked; the rest stays.
  • Share your list as text when someone else is doing the run.

More

  • Purchase History and Past Trips show what you bought.
  • Backup & Transfer moves your data to a new phone.
  • Everything stays on this device. No account.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. ListAhead has no account and no server. If iCloud Drive is on, a backup is also saved to your own iCloud.

How do I move my list to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud Drive turned on, install ListAhead and open it. It offers to restore your iCloud backup. You can also go to More → Backup & Transfer → Restore from iCloud.

Can I keep my own backup file?

Yes. Go to More → Backup & Transfer to export a backup file, and restore it from the same screen.

Why don’t I see suggestions in Might need soon yet?

ListAhead needs to see an item bought at least 4 times before it can tell how often you buy it. Keep using Finish shopping, and suggestions appear on their own.

A store I added doesn’t show on Home.

Stores appear on Home once they have items. Add an item and set this store as its Preferred Store or under Also available at.

How do I remove an item or store?

Go to More → Items or More → Stores and swipe to archive it. Archived items and stores can be restored from the same screen.
