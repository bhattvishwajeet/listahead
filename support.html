ListAhead Support

ListAhead is a grocery list that learns how you shop. It organizes your list by store, shows you where to start, and suggests what you might need next.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email listahead.app@gmail.com. I usually reply within two business days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. ListAhead has no account and no server. If iCloud Drive is on, a backup is also saved to your own iCloud.

How do I move my list to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud Drive turned on, install ListAhead and open it. It offers to restore your iCloud backup. You can also go to More → Backup & Transfer → Restore from iCloud.

Can I keep my own backup file?

Yes. Go to More → Backup & Transfer to export a backup file, and restore it from the same screen.

How does Might need soon work?

After you've bought an item a few times, ListAhead learns how often you buy it and suggests it when it's due.

What does Start at mean?

It's the store that covers the most items on your list, so you know where to go first.

Can I use it for chores?

Yes. Add a store called Household Chores, add chores as items, and ListAhead learns how often each one comes around.
