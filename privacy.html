Privacy Policy

ListAhead · Last updated October 28, 2026

ListAhead does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

What ListAhead stores

The items, stores, shopping lists and purchase history you enter are stored on your iPhone. ListAhead has no account, no server and no way to see your data.

iCloud backup

If iCloud Drive is on, ListAhead saves a backup file to your own iCloud Drive so you can restore it on a new iPhone. This file is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and is not accessible to the developer. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Files and sharing

When you export a backup or share your list, ListAhead uses the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes. When you restore from a file, ListAhead only opens the file you pick. It doesn't read your photos or other files.

No analytics, ads or tracking

ListAhead contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and doesn't use any third-party services that collect data.

Children

ListAhead doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
